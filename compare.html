How to map your holdings

The world portfolio is published in seven sleeves. Cash is not part of the investable market portfolio, so its world weight is zero; it is included here only so that your inputs can sum to 100.

SleeveTypical holdings
Global equityListed shares and equity funds of any country, developed or emerging, including REITs
DM government bondsNominal bonds issued by developed-market central governments (Treasuries, Bunds, JGBs, gilts) and funds holding them
CreditCorporate and bank bonds, including covered bonds, and bond funds holding them; investment grade and high yield alike (published as one sleeve)
SecuritisedMortgage-backed and asset-backed securities, including agency mortgage pass-throughs and CLOs, and funds holding them
EM debtBonds issued by emerging-market governments and companies, in any currency
Inflation-linkedInflation-indexed government bonds (TIPS, index-linked gilts, JGBi) and funds holding them
GoldBullion, coins, and physically backed gold funds
CashDeposits, money-market funds, and bills under one year

A balanced fund should be split into its sleeves using its published allocation. Private assets, cryptoassets, commodities other than gold, and your own home are outside the measured universe and have no world weight here; leave them out or add them to cash, and read the result accordingly.

What the comparison is, and is not

The world weights are those of the current CC-GMP Index issue: market values in US dollars, gross of costs, estimated from public data within the ten disclosed limitations. They are a measurement of what all investors together hold, chosen by no one. The comparison therefore shows where your allocation departs from that aggregate. It does not say whether the departure is right for you, and nothing on this page is a recommendation. The weights are stated in US-dollar terms; no claim is made about any other base currency.
