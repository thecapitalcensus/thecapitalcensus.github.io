The Capital Census

A measurement of what the world's investors actually hold — estimated from public data, with the method, the calibration record, and the corrections all published alongside the figures.

Estimates of the global market portfolio exist, but their construction is not disclosed. This project takes the opposite position. Every input is public, every step is documented, and every revision stays on the record — including results that ran against our own hypotheses.

The index takes no discretionary bets. Its weights are the aggregate holdings of all investors, chosen by no one.

Capital Census Global Market Portfolio Index

Short form: CC-GMP Index. Published monthly. The index reports the composition and size of the investable global market portfolio; it is not a price index and has no level.

Release note — No. 1

This is the first official issue. It publishes seven sleeves. The rulebook calculates eight: investment-grade credit and high yield are reported here as one combined credit sleeve, because the parameter that separates them (A3) is uncalibrated and every free route to calibrating it has failed. Publishing the split as a measurement would claim a precision the data does not support, so it is carried as a labelled auxiliary estimate instead.

Nothing has been revised this month, because there is nothing prior to revise. From the next issue onward this note will state what changed, what was re-examined, and what did not confirm what we expected — on the same terms as results that did.

What remains unresolved, stated here rather than in a footnote. A3 is uncalibrated: no rating-level split of outstanding balances exists in free primary data, and the attempt record is published in Methodology §4. Central-bank holdings are deducted for three institutions only — the Federal Reserve, the ECB and the Bank of Japan — so holdings of the Bank of England, the People's Bank of China and others remain inside the figures. BIS statistics cover 53 countries, which biases the bond block toward underestimation. The full list is the ten disclosed limitations, and they should be read before any figure here is used.

The estimation engine behind these figures was not adopted for weight publication on its own terms; what was frozen and published first was the methodology. Seven-sleeve publication was authorised separately by the third revision of the rulebook. That sequence — methodology first, figures second, and only in the form the calibration actually supports — is the intended one.

What is disclosed

All rules, calibrations, and known limitations are disclosed in full, including results unfavorable to our own hypotheses. Ten limitations define the boundary within which any figure here may be read; they are listed in Methodology, and readers are asked to read them before using any figure published on this site.
