The Capital Census

A measurement of what the world's investors actually hold — estimated from public data, with the method, the calibration record, and the corrections all published alongside the figures.

Estimates of the global market portfolio exist, but their construction is not disclosed. This project takes the opposite position. Every input is public, every step is documented, and every revision stays on the record — including results that ran against our own hypotheses.

The index takes no discretionary bets. Its weights are the aggregate holdings of all investors, chosen by no one.
