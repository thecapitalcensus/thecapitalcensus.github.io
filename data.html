50-Year Weight History of the Investable Global Market Portfolio

1976–2026 · USD-denominated · Capital Census, Data Release D

A citable dataset covering fifty years of estimated weights for the investable global market portfolio. It is released as a standalone research object and serves as the supporting material for the project's papers.

All figures published here are the author's own calculations from publicly available data. Sources are cited where used for calibration; no third-party proprietary data is redistributed. Estimates are subject to the limitations disclosed in the methodology (including survivorship, proxy, and coverage limitations). Errors, when found, are corrected with a visible revision history rather than silent edits.

What the rows mean

Weights are annual, measured at each year-end from 1975 to 2025. A row dated 1975-12-31 is the reconstruction target for January 1976, which is why a 1975–2025 measurement series produces a 1976–2026 portfolio history. Weights are percentages rounded to four decimal places; each row sums to exactly 100.0000, with the rounding residual absorbed by the largest sleeve.

The official series publishes seven sleeves, with investment-grade credit and high yield combined. The auxiliary file splits them, but that split is a fixed ratio carried across every year rather than a year-by-year observation, and carries no time-series information. The same caution applies to the inflation-linked share.

Conditions of disclosure

Three constraints define what this series is. They are properties of the data itself, not caveats about its use, and any reading of the figures must take them as given.

  1. Securitised debt is anchored to the frozen v3 calibration. The securitised-debt sleeve uses the frozen v3 calibration values rather than being re-estimated period by period.
  2. High-yield and inflation-linked shares are held fixed. Within the bond sleeves, these shares are held at their fixed calibrated structure across the full history.
  3. Central bank holdings cover three institutions only. Holdings of the Federal Reserve, the ECB, and the Bank of Japan are deducted; other official-sector holdings are not netted out.

Users who require different treatments should re-derive weights from the disclosed methodology rather than adjust these figures ad hoc.

What this is not

  • Not investment advice, not a product, not a solicitation.
  • Not a claim of performance superiority over any benchmark. Over our 50-year verification (1976–2026), the annualized return difference versus a 60/40 portfolio was within ±0.88 percentage points at equal risk (90% confidence interval), this being the least favourable of the three 60/40 bond-leg definitions examined.
  • All results are USD-denominated. No claims are made for other numeraires.
